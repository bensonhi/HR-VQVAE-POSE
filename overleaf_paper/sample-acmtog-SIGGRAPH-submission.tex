\begin{abstract}
Generating natural co-speech gestures requires precise temporal alignment, semantic relevance, and seamless coordination between the face, body, and hands.
Current methods often struggle with long-form coherence and fail to generalize across different speakers.
We introduce GestureCraft, a hierarchical framework for synthesizing full-body SMPL-X gestures directly from speech.
Our architecture utilizes a Transformer VAE with a continuous latent space partitioned into distinct slices for the face, body, and hands.
This structure is supervised by part-specific decoding losses to ensure kinematic integrity.
To generate high-fidelity motion, we employ a conditional flow-matching prior that integrates speaker speech, identity, and gesture intent.
We maintain long-term stability through anchor-prefix conditioning and continuation velocity loss, which enforces smoothness across sequence boundaries.
Furthermore, we leverage a fine-tuned LLM to plan gesture segments from speaker transcripts.
Evaluated on the BEAT2 dataset, GestureCraft achieves state-of-the-art performance and demonstrates robust cross-speaker generalization.
\end{abstract}
